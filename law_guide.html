Top 10 Reasons People Hire a Lawyer

Most situations involve high stress or money. Here is why people call for help:

  • 1. Criminal Defense: To stay out of jail and protect rights.
  • 2. Divorce/Family Law: For custody and fair asset division.
  • 3. Personal Injury: To get insurance companies to pay for medical bills.
  • 4. Wills & Estate Planning: To make sure inheritance goes to the right people.
  • 5. Real Estate: Reviewing contracts for buying/selling homes.
  • 6. Business Formation: Setting up LLCs and protecting owners from lawsuits.
  • 7. Employment Disputes: Dealing with wrongful firing or harassment.
  • 8. Bankruptcy: To stop creditors and get a fresh financial start.
  • 9. Traffic Tickets: When a license or insurance rate is at risk.
  • 10. Contract Fights: When someone breaks a signed agreement.
